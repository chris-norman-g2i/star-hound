STARHOUND — NEON RUN
A static Three.js space runner. No package manager, build step, backend, or application server.

GITHUB PAGES
Extract starhound-github-pages.zip into your repository root (or copy the contents of this directory).
Commit index.html, style.css, js/, README.txt and .nojekyll.
On GitHub, open Settings > Pages. Select "Deploy from a branch", choose your branch and "/ (root)", then Save.
The published page runs directly. All URLs are relative, so repository subpaths work.
No Actions build or npm commands are required. An internet connection is needed for the pinned Three.js CDN import and Google Fonts; system fonts remain available if font loading fails.
ES modules require HTTP(S) hosting. Double-clicking index.html via file:// is restricted by browser module security.

CONTROLS
WASD or arrows: steer inside the tunnel
Space: hold to fire
Shift: boost using the draining speed-charge meter
R: reload early
Esc / P: pause or resume
M: toggle music and sound
Enter: launch from the title screen
The game automatically pauses when its tab is hidden or its window loses focus.

FLIGHT
10 sectors x 5 waves = 50 levels. Cruising takes roughly 20 minutes; boosts shorten the race.
Shoot drones and rocks. Orange barriers are indestructible: steer through the remaining corridor.
Drones begin firing in later waves and become tougher and more mobile.
Enemies always drop a pickup. Additional pickups spawn along the course.
Cannon upgrades progress 1 > 2 > 4 > 6 barrels. Fire and reload upgrades reach level IV.
Heat cools over time. After overheating, the cannon remains locked until heat reaches zero plus one extra second.
A hollow, checkered square gate appears at the end of wave 50. Fly through its center to win; missing the opening loses the race.

MODULES
js/game.js: lifecycle and frame pipeline; no procedural calculations in start/update.
js/settings.js: all tuning and authored math for race, flight, tunnel, assets, graphics, particles, cannon, pickups, sound effects, and music.
js/systems.js: runtime encounter, projectile, damage and pickup orchestration.
js/scene.js: Three.js resources, polygon meshes, shared dog/starship, curved tunnel and instanced polygon particles.
js/sound.js: Web Audio synthesis, stereo voices, drums, speed-responsive engine/wind, noise, filters, delay, reverb, compression and look-ahead scheduling.
js/interface.js: title, manual, HUD, pause, defeat and victory UI.

ORIGINAL MUSIC (7 TRACKS)
Soft Launch — mellow intro, 78 BPM
Copper Funk — syncopated minor/Dorian bass and plucks
Glass Arcade — bright Lydian glass arpeggios
Afterburn Velvet — broken-beat minor synth leads
Solar Disco — major disco bass and bright harmonies
Goodboy Forever — invincibility/victory future synth, fixed 104 BPM
Drifting Home — sad downtempo defeat, 62 BPM
Main tracks have 16-bar harmonic cycles and four-bar ascending 16th/32nd flares; their tempo follows current ship speed.
Music is generated by Web Audio, with no audio file downloads. Audio is unlocked by a player gesture.
