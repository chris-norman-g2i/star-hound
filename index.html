YOUR CANNON

Infinite ammunition. Find 2, 4, then 6 barrels. Fire-rate and cooling upgrades reach level IV. Overheating locks your cannon until it cools completely, plus 0.5 seconds. Feather your fire to stay ready.

LIVES & CHECKPOINTS

Start with three lives. A lost life restarts the current wave with full hull and a three-second shield. Your upgrades, charge, kills and score return to their wave-start checkpoint values; flight time keeps counting. Every wave begins with a quiet, bonus-rich stretch. There is no victory screen or finish line.

Wave checkpoints are retained automatically when browser storage is available. Resume from checkpoint in the hangar. A new run clears the previous checkpoint. Losing all three lives ends the run and clears its checkpoint.
