FLIGHT LAB

DEVELOPER SETTINGS.

Apply saves these settings for new flights and checkpoint resumes. Return to the hangar to change them. Play Now applies the form and starts a fresh flight at the selected wave, replacing your checkpoint.

Difficulty grows continuously with distance. Growth distance sets the scale; the multiplier controls its rate (0 keeps starting difficulty), and the exponent shapes the curve. Speed, obstacle count, health, drift and fire rate use this shared progression.

Minimum pickup spacing applies everywhere, including bonus stretches and enemy drops. Frequency is the chance at each eligible opportunity: 0 disables pickups, 1 uses every opportunity that satisfies the minimum gap.

Asteroid size sets the base radius. Size variability multiplies it independently for each asteroid (default 0.6–1.6×). Each asteroid has its own irregular surface with random dents and bulges. Asteroids pack randomly around their center of mass, favoring nearby positions while keeping the specified surface gap at spawn. Quantity determines how many belong to a cluster. The center-distance range places each cluster at a random angle around the track. Clusters spawn 1,000 units ahead. Each instance samples its values once. Tumble uses radians per second; velocity uses units per second in a fixed random direction.

Traffic can merge into your path or approach head-on. Vehicle impacts cost 60% hull; pileup wreckage costs 15%. Close passes draw angry horns. Movie crossings include glass, strawberry carts and stacked boxes (10% hull each), plus clothing that briefly sticks to the pilot and slows the ship.

Open space share controls the distance spent in open space. Motif weights control their relative frequency; a weight of zero disables that motif. Station openings start larger and shrink toward their minimum dimensions with difficulty. Their positions span the track while remaining fully contained. Chains contain up to three walls with small, reachable opening shifts; wall spacing increases with speed to allow steering time. Ring bonuses decay gradually and are lost or reduced by collisions.
