STARHOUND TITLE ASSETS

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the user's supplied
hangar painting as the visual reference. No image-generation CLI was used.
The original painting is preserved in hangar-reference.png.

Runtime assets
- hangar-clean-v1.png: static 1672 x 941 background.
- scarf-wind-v2.png: transparent eight-frame atlas, four columns and two rows.
- tail-wind-v2.png: transparent eight-frame atlas, four columns and two rows.
Earlier v1 atlases are retained as intermediate references.

Prompt set / edit briefs

Clean background:
Remove the entire orange scarf, including its neck band and flowing end, and
the entire fluffy curled tail. Reconstruct the exposed flight suit, starship
and background. Preserve the dog's face, body, helmet and hands, the ship,
logo, menu, camera and lighting. Preserve the native 1672 x 941 composition.

Scarf animation:
Create exactly eight transparent low-poly orange scarf frames in a four-column,
two-row atlas. Pin the left neck attachment in every frame. Use stronger wind:
a pronounced traveling S wave, tip rise/fall around 40-60 pixels per cell,
billowing folds and twists. Lift, flatten, dip and recover in a seamless loop.
Keep scale, camera and anchor consistent; retain padding and clean alpha edges.

Tail animation:
Create exactly eight transparent gray-and-cream husky tail frames in a
four-column, two-row atlas. Pin the dark left root in every frame. Use stronger
wind: upper curl sway around 15-25 degrees, tip whip around 30-45 pixels and
streaming fur. Bend the free part with a stationary root and a seamless loop.
Keep scale, camera, lighting and registration consistent, with clean alpha edges.
The web layer separately makes the tail 20% narrower and 20% longer.

Playback
Two aligned DIVs use the atlases over the clean background. Settings, anchors
and scale are centralized in js/settings.js under gfx.hangar.
Eight frames at 0.24 seconds each give a 1.92-second loop, 2.5 times faster
than the original 0.6-second frame timing. Reduced motion holds a static frame.
