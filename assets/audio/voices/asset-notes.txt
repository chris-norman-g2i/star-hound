STARHOUND CHARACTER VOICES — 2026-10-07

16 user-authored phrases, synthesized locally with Kokoro-82M v1.0.
Hero: am_michael, speed 0.93, with a gentle 4% pitch reduction and warm EQ.
Computer: af_heart, speed 1.02, with restrained filtering and a quiet 32ms echo.
Final files: mono MP3, 24kHz, 96kbps; matched to -18 LUFS, true-peak target -2.5dB.
Speech tails and internal pauses are preserved; only leading/trailing silence is trimmed.
Exact supplied dialogue is recorded in manifest.json. Per-line synthesisText and
speed overrides improve interjections and short phrases: Yee-haw drops its
hyphen, the close-one line uses an ellipsis, and the good-boy question uses
question intonation. Dialogue wording is retained.
Automatic recognition is an aid, not a guarantee of intelligibility; short
interjections and the good-boy praise lines need a listener's audition.

GENERATION & SOURCE
Model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
ONNX conversion: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
Library: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/tree/main/kokoro.js
kokoro-js 1.2.1; Transformers.js 3.8.1; full-precision CPU inference.
Model/library license: Apache-2.0; a copy is included in LICENSE-Kokoro.txt.
No model weights, speech service, cloned speaker or inference library is shipped
with the game. Playback uses the generated local MP3 files only.

DEVELOPMENT
work/voices/generate.mjs regenerates the clips from manifest.json.
work/voices/preview.mjs builds preview.html and both output/audio previews.
work/voices/raw keeps the unprocessed WAV masters.
work/voices/transcribe.mjs checks rendered clips using local Whisper recognition.
Generation dependencies and model downloads live in /private/tmp/starhound-kokoro.
They can be reinstalled there; the deployed game needs none of them.

PLAYBACK
New run: Ready for launch, followed by Bark bark, mother truckers.
Continue: Checkpoint resumed, followed by Get that bone.
Wave boundary: Checkpoint reached.
Lost life: Ship, lost, followed by Checkpoint resumed when the ship respawns.
Final lost life: Ship, lost.
Hero reactions cover hits, low hull, enemy kills, powered pickups and near misses.
Computer praise plays occasionally on pickups and safe bonus stretches.
One clip plays at a time. Life-loss announcements preempt older dialogue;
checkpoint announcements preempt casual quips. Casual lines share a 14s cooldown.
M mutes voices with all sound; pause retains the current position; the hangar
clears pending lines. Music/effects soften during speech and recover afterward.
Volume, ducking, spacing, event thresholds and paths are in settings.voices.
